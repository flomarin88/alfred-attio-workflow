# Input reconciliation: brain dump

## Input summary

Florian's opening message (in French) framed the project as an Alfred (macOS) workflow for **all Attio CRM users**, whose purpose is to let users **access the CRM directly from their Mac without opening the app** — specifically: list of companies, list of tasks-to-do, **create new tasks**, **create new companies**, **create new clients** — and thereby **gain productivity** ("permet de gagner en productivité"). Three reference URLs were given (Attio help, Attio docs, Alfred workflows) plus the explicit build choice of `fast-alfred` (Avivbens) as the development framework. The dump is short but carries five concrete capabilities, one architectural choice, one audience framing ("tous les utilisateurs"), and one outcome phrase ("gagner en productivité").

## What landed cleanly

- **"Workflow Alfred (MacOs) ... pour tous les utilisateurs du CRM Attio"** — surfaced in §1 Context and §3 Users. The role-neutral framing in §3 ("sales reps, account executives, customer success, founders") is consistent with "tous les utilisateurs".
- **"Accéder ... sans avoir ouvert l'application"** — captured as the thesis in §1 ("collapse the switch-to-Attio pattern into 1–3 keystrokes") and operationalised through UJ-1, UJ-2, UJ-4.
- **"Liste des entreprises"** — companies search keyword in F-B (FR-010, FR-013, FR-020) and Quick Look/drill-down for company in addendum §E.
- **"Liste des tâches à faire"** — `todo` keyword is the headline of F-A (FR-001 through FR-009), anchored in UJ-1, with overdue grouping, deadline sort, mark-complete inline.
- **"J'utilise fast-alfred pour développer le workflow"** — addendum §B is built entirely around fast-alfred primitives (`alfredClient.cache`, `alfredClient.env.getEnv`, `alfredClient.config`, `alfredClient.icons`, `alfredClient.output`, `fast-alfred pack`).
- **Documentation references** (Attio help, Attio docs, Alfred workflows) — implicitly honored by the API surface table (addendum §A) and by the fast-alfred framing (§B); the URLs themselves are not cited inline but the technical decisions track them.

## What transformed (and whether the transformation holds up)

- **"Créer des nouvelles tâches" / "créer des nouvelles entreprises" / "créer des nouveaux clients"** → transformed into a **V1 non-goal (NG1)** and pushed to **V2-1**. The PRD names the transformation explicitly (multi-step input UX, attribute discovery, conflict handling exceed V1's design budget) and substitutes an _update_ surface (F-G) for existing records. **Holds up at the engineering-budget level, but the transformation is substantial**: the brain dump put creation at the _centre_ of the productivity story (three of the five concrete asks), and V1 ships zero creation. The PRD compensates with F-G (in-place edits) and presents F-G as a co-headline surface with read, but a reasonable reader of the brain dump would expect "create task" to be in V1.
- **"Créer des nouveaux clients"** → transformed into **NG5** ("client" is a status on a person, not a native Attio object) plus per-workspace lifecycle-attribute configuration (FR-033). **Holds up technically** — the decision is grounded in Attio's data model and there's a decision-log entry — but it silently re-frames a user-named noun ("client") into an attribute on `person`. The reader who said "créer des nouveaux clients" wanted a one-keystroke way to add a customer to the CRM; what they get in V1 is the ability to _update_ an existing person's lifecycle attribute. Two transformations stacked on the same noun: noun→attribute, and create→update.
- **"Productivité" / productivity gain** → transformed into a thesis statement in §1 ("collapse the switch-to-Attio pattern into 1–3 keystrokes") and a **named validation gap** in §7 (no quantitative metric for thesis validation in V1; community-signal proxies only). **Holds up as an honest engineering trade-off** — the PRD is explicit that the productivity claim cannot be measured under NFR-009 (no telemetry). The user said "productivité" once; the PRD names it as the central thesis and then names that it is unverifiable in V1. That is a sound transformation as long as the user accepts the trade-off knowingly.
- **"Sans avoir ouvert l'application"** → transformed into UJ-2 closing line ("they have not seen Attio's UI") and UJ-4 ("they have not seen Attio's UI") and into the §1 thesis. **Holds up.** This implicit quality became the load-bearing user experience for V1.

## What dropped (and whether the drop is explicit or silent)

- **Creation flows for tasks and companies** — **explicitly dropped** in NG1 with reasoning, and **explicitly scheduled** in V2-1. This is the cleanest possible "drop": named, justified, and routed to a future version. Worth flagging only because the brain dump put it front-and-centre.
- **"Liste des entreprises" as a standalone surface** — partially **silently dropped**. The brain dump lists "list of companies" alongside "list of tasks" as a peer first-class surface. The PRD makes `todo` (tasks) a dedicated headline keyword with its own user journey (UJ-1) but does _not_ give companies the same treatment. Companies is reached only through the `company` search keyword (FR-010, FR-020) with empty-query fallback to recently-updated. There is no "morning companies" journey, no equivalent of UJ-1 for companies. The transformation is reasonable (a static "list of all companies" in a 9-row Alfred bar is not actually useful for prospecting), but the demotion from peer surface to search-keyword-with-empty-query is **not named anywhere in the PRD** — a reader of the brain dump would not see this delta surfaced.
- **"Pour tous les utilisateurs du CRM Attio" as a community framing** — **silently softened**. The brain dump's "tous les utilisateurs" reads as inclusive ("every Attio user, regardless of role") and gestures at a community / open-source intent. §3 acknowledges role-neutrality but does not pick up the "community" implication. The open-source distribution model is named (G3, NFR-013, NFR-014, NFR-016, the GitHub Releases pipeline) but the _audience-as-community_ framing is not — there's no positioning paragraph about who this is for as a community, no mention of contributor pathway, no "Alfred power-user community + Attio user community" intersection language. The README rewrite (NFR-014) and Gallery submission (addendum §G) are the places this would surface; both are TODO.
- **The three reference URLs from the brain dump** — **silently dropped from the body** of the PRD. Attio's help / docs / Alfred workflows pages are not cited as references in the PRD or addendum. The technical decisions they informed (API endpoints, workflow distribution mechanics) are present, but the provenance trail is not. Minor for the build, notable for a reader trying to retrace the decision-making.
- **The phrase "productivité"** as a literal user-visible artefact — **silently dropped**. The thesis paraphrases it as "collapse the switch-to-Attio pattern into 1–3 keystrokes" but the word "productivity" doesn't appear in the §1 problem framing as the user-named outcome. This is a tone/voice nit, not a capability gap, but the user did name the outcome explicitly and the PRD chose a different vocabulary for it.

## Gaps worth surfacing

1. **Creation is V1 in the brain dump, V2 in the PRD — and three of the five concrete brain-dump asks live there.** (Major.) The transformation is explicit and reasoned, but the scope reduction is large enough that the PRD should probably check this with the user before V1 ships rather than discover it at release. A V1 that ships zero creation may surprise the originator who emphasised "créer ... créer ... créer ...". Worth a one-line explicit acknowledgement in §1 ("the brain dump asked for creation; V1 ships only read+update; here's why").

2. **"Client" semantics quietly stacked two transformations.** (Notable.) NG5 handles the _noun_ transformation (client → status on person), and NG1 handles the _verb_ transformation (create → update). Both individually defensible, but their composition means the brain-dump phrase "créer des nouveaux clients" maps to "update an existing person's lifecycle attribute slug, which you must configure manually in FR-033." That is a long way from the user's original wording. The README / onboarding copy should call this out so the user (and other Attio users) aren't surprised.

3. **Companies were demoted from peer surface to search-with-empty-query fallback, silently.** (Notable.) The brain dump put "liste des entreprises" and "liste des tâches" on equal footing. The PRD gives tasks a dedicated journey and a dedicated keyword (`todo`); companies share the four-keyword search surface with people/deals/tasks. This may be the right product call, but the delta should be named — either by adding a `companies` headline equivalent of `todo`, or by stating in §2 that the brain-dump's "list of companies" intent is served by `company` + empty-query and explaining why that's enough.

4. **The "community / for all Attio users" framing is present in audience phrasing but absent in positioning.** (Notable.) The PRD's §3 says role-neutral; the brain dump said "tous les utilisateurs du CRM Attio" which carries a stronger community signal. The open-source distribution choices honor it implicitly but never name it as positioning. The README (NFR-014, currently TODO) is the natural place to land this — a one-paragraph "who this is for, and why it's free and open" framing would close the loop.

5. **"Productivité" as the user-named outcome is paraphrased away.** (Minor.) The PRD chose precise engineering language ("collapse the switch-to-Attio pattern into 1–3 keystrokes") which is good for builders but loses the user's word. The §7 success-metrics section is honest that the productivity claim can't be measured; that honesty is good. But the user-facing surfaces (README, `info.plist` description NFR-015, Gallery submission copy) should probably keep "productivity" as the headline word to honor the originating voice.
