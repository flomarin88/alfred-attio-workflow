# Prose review — prd.md

## Overall voice assessment

The voice is largely consistent: neutral-technical, declarative, terse where it counts. It drifts in three predictable directions. First, §1 and §3 occasionally slip toward marketing register ("collapse the 'switch to Attio to do one small thing' pattern", "quietly degrades", "well-designed Alfred workflow") that reads as pitch rather than spec. Second, several FR/NFR paragraphs grow long subordinate-clause tails — especially FR-004, FR-033, FR-051, NFR-006a, NFR-009, NFR-010 — that force the reader to parse a compound thought where two declarative sentences would land cleaner. Third, the UJ sections mix present-tense narration ("They hit `⌥space`, type `todo`...") with future-flavored modal phrasings ("On a given task they can:") and a few subjunctive lapses ("would be a more deliberate thesis check" in §7). None of these are fatal; they're sentence-level cleanups.

## Hedging / declarative drift

- **§1 ¶1** "Repeated dozens of times a day this friction quietly degrades both productivity and CRM hygiene" → "Repeated dozens of times a day, this friction degrades productivity and CRM hygiene." — `quietly` is a hedge softener with no spec value.
- **§1 ¶2** "A well-designed Alfred workflow can collapse..." → "An Alfred workflow collapses..." — `well-designed` is self-praise and `can` hedges what the PRD is committing to deliver.
- **NFR-001** "feel instant" and "with no perceptible spinner" — "feel" and "perceptible" are intentional UX softeners but read as hedges in an NFR. Consider "render in a single frame after Enter; no spinner is shown."
- **NFR-002** "complete in under a couple of seconds on a normal home or office connection" → "complete in under two seconds on a typical broadband connection." — "a couple of" and "normal" are imprecise.
- **NFR-014** "macOS version floor TBD at build" and "license declaration (TBD at build, see §9)" — `TBD` is acceptable for build-time slots but the parenthetical "see §9" is correct for license only; verify TBD scope.
- **§7 ¶ Qualitative validation** "would be a more deliberate thesis check" → "is a more deliberate thesis check." — the conditional softens a stated V2 prep item.
- **§8 ¶2** "Build-time decisions that the PRD intentionally does not bind" — "intentionally" is rhetorical; the sentence stands without it.
- **NFR-006a** "users who rapid-fire the same mutation may emit duplicate writes, which is Attio's idempotency contract to handle" — `may` is correct here (actually conditional), but "which is Attio's idempotency contract to handle" is awkward; see Awkward Phrasing.
- **FR-027** "Multi-workspace is not supported in V1" is good; but UJ-3 closing paragraph "Users who work across multiple Attio workspaces need to either pick one for V1 or wait for V2's OAuth flow" → "Users who work across multiple Attio workspaces must pick one for V1 or wait for V2's OAuth flow." — `need to either … or` is wordier than `must … or`.

## Awkward phrasing

- **§1 ¶1** "spend most of their day in surfaces other than the CRM: inboxes, calendars, chat apps, prospect websites, doc tools" → "spend most of their day outside the CRM — in inboxes, calendars, chat apps, prospect websites, doc tools." — "in surfaces other than" is clunky.
- **§1 ¶1** "switching to it costs a context flip: tab-find or app-launch, page-load, search, click" — comma-stacking after a colon list reads OK but the parallelism breaks ("tab-find or app-launch" vs single nouns). Consider "tab-find, app-launch, page-load, search, click."
- **§1 ¶3** "V1 has two co-headline surfaces" — "co-headline" is jargon. → "V1 has two headline surfaces."
- **G1** "list, search, and inspect their own daily work and the workspace's records (people, companies, deals, tasks) without leaving the Alfred bar" — compound object is hard to parse. Consider splitting: "list, search, and inspect the workspace's records (people, companies, deals, tasks) — including their own daily work — without leaving the Alfred bar."
- **G2** "so the workflow is not purely a navigator and saves the most painful CRM-hygiene round-trips" — two ideas joined with `and`; tighten to "so the workflow goes beyond navigation and removes the most painful CRM-hygiene round-trips."
- **NG1** "creation requires multi-step input UX, per-workspace attribute discovery for required-vs-optional fields, and conflict handling — all of which exceed V1's design budget" — long; fine but consider splitting the trailing clause.
- **NG5** "The brain dump's 'créer des nouveaux clients' resolves in V1 to 'find an existing person and update their lifecycle attribute slug'" — "the brain dump's" is internal-process language that leaks into the PRD. Consider "The intent 'créer des nouveaux clients' resolves..."
- **UJ-1 step 4** "They clear two tasks, open one to read context, then close Alfred and move on." — fine, but "move on" is colloquial. Consider "...then close Alfred." (drop "and move on").
- **UJ-2 ¶1** "anything but Attio" — fine; "(or Slack, or a doc — anything but Attio)" could read "(Slack, a doc, anywhere but Attio)" but this is taste; leave.
- **UJ-2 step 3 ⌥⏎** "the query field becomes the note body, Enter creates an Attio Note linked to this record" — comma splice. → "the query field becomes the note body; Enter creates an Attio Note linked to this record."
- **UJ-3 step 6** "On this first successful API call, the workflow probes the identity endpoint to resolve workspace ID + slug + workspace member ID, caches these as 'me', and shows a one-time macOS notification" — long sentence with three coordinated verbs; split: "On this first successful API call, the workflow probes the identity endpoint to resolve workspace ID, slug, and workspace member ID. It caches these as 'me' and shows a one-time macOS notification."
- **UJ-3 closing ¶** "This V1 flow is honest about its constraints" — "honest" is editorializing. Consider "This V1 flow has clear constraints."
- **UJ-3 closing ¶** "The README must make this clear up-front so the constraint doesn't surface as a frustration." — "surface as a frustration" is awkward. → "...so the constraint doesn't frustrate users later."
- **UJ-4 step 4** "on success it invalidates the deal's cached entry, re-fetches it, and re-renders the drill-down with the new value; a macOS notification confirms 'Deal updated: Acme Series A → Negotiation'" — long; split at the semicolon as two sentences for breathing room.
- **FR-004** "On contract break the workflow silently degrades to opening the tasks list view; it does not detect the break itself." — "silently degrades" pairs awkwardly with "does not detect the break itself" (the second clause makes "silently" redundant since detection is impossible). Suggest: "If the URL pattern breaks, the workflow degrades to opening the tasks list view. It cannot detect the break itself."
- **FR-005** "the notification surfaces a workflow-owned error string (FR-051) — not raw Attio error text — and the list is not modified" — em-dash sandwich plus passive tail. → "the notification surfaces a workflow-owned error string (FR-051) instead of raw Attio error text. The list is not modified."
- **FR-015** "For deal/task this mod degrades to opening in Attio with a hint shown the first time ⌘⏎ is invoked on a deal or task." — "this mod" is jargon and "with a hint shown" trails awkwardly. → "For deal/task, ⌘⏎ degrades to opening the record in Attio; a hint is shown the first time ⌘⏎ is invoked on a deal or task."
- **FR-015 trailing ¶ (orphaned)** "Note creation, shared rules (FR-016 and FR-017): UTF-8 content, `format=plaintext` (no markdown)..." — this stand-alone paragraph sits between FR-015 and FR-016 without its own ID. Reads as a stray header. (Structural — flagged for awareness; not editing.)
- **FR-022** "The result framing is deliberately not 'setup guide' (which would imply a wizard) because the actual flow requires the user to paste a value into Alfred's native config sheet, which Alfred has no API to open from a script." — 40+ words, two `which` clauses. The actual flow requires the user to paste a value into Alfred's native config sheet, and Alfred provides no API to open it from a script."
- **FR-025** "On this first probe's success, a one-time macOS notification confirms 'Connected to <workspace_name>' so the user has a closing receipt for the onboarding loop." — "closing receipt for the onboarding loop" is metaphor that doesn't pay off. → "...so the user gets confirmation that onboarding succeeded."
- **FR-033** "Tasks are intentionally excluded from this config because their relevant lifecycle (`is_completed`) is already rendered in FR-013's task context line as a boolean." — fine but long; "intentionally" is editorializing.

Split: "All workflow-rendered error notifications use the workflow's own English/French strings (FR-037). The exception is HTTP 422 validation errors on `PATCH /v2/objects/...`: Attio's field-level message is surfaced verbatim, because translating would lose precision."
- **NFR-004** "Why these values: tasks change most often per user (60s balances freshness vs request count for the `todo` keyword); deals get hand-edited during sales motion (5min); people are mostly stable but profile edits happen (10min); companies are the most stable in normal CRM use, dominated by re-reads of the same firm during prospecting (24h)." — semicolon list works, but "deals get hand-edited" and "profile edits happen" are vague. - **NFR-006a** "users who rapid-fire the same mutation may emit duplicate writes, which is Attio's idempotency contract to handle" → "users who rapid-fire the same mutation may emit duplicate writes; handling that is Attio's idempotency contract."
- **NFR-007** "(the affordances render but ⏎/⌘⏎ surface 'Cannot reach Attio — try again later' without sending the request)" — parenthetical inside a parenthetical-feeling clause. Promote to its own sentence.
- **NFR-010** "Note: `alfredClient.config` (the `sindresorhus/conf` store used for cached identity / workspace slug / `cmd_enter_hint_dismissed` / token-hash) writes plaintext JSON to `~/Library/Preferences/`; nothing sensitive lands there." — long parenthetical with slash-list. - **NFR-014** "The README, currently a `TODO` placeholder, must be authored before V1.0.0: install steps (Alfred 5+, macOS version floor TBD at build), screenshots, keyword reference for every keyword in F-A through F-G, PAT-generation walkthrough with required scopes, troubleshooting, known limitations (single-workspace, no fuzzy search, task deep-link best-effort), and license declaration (TBD at build, see §9)." — single 60+ word sentence with three nested parentheticals. ## Inconsistent terminology

- **drill-down vs sub-list vs sub-script-filter** — UJ-1 step 3 calls it "drill down" then "sub-script-filter"; UJ-2 step 3 says "drill-down (single-level): a sub-script-filter"; UJ-4 step 2 says "sub-list"; FR-007 says "drill-down script filter"; FR-019 says "drill-down" and "nested drill-down"; FR-042 says "sub-script-filter" and "sub-search". The glossary defines **Drill-down** as "a sub-script-filter the workflow opens on **→**". Pick one user-facing term ("drill-down") and one technical term ("sub-script-filter") and use each consistently — `sub-list` should be replaced with `drill-down`.
- **`web_url` vs API-returned `web_url`** — UJ-2 step 3 says "uses the API-returned `web_url`"; FR-014 uses the same phrase; FR-004 references "no API-returned `web_url`". Glossary defines `web_url`. Consistent — no change.
- **"open the record in Attio" vs "open in Attio"** — both forms appear (UJ-1 step 3, FR-014, FR-015, FR-044). Minor inconsistency, fine if kept.
- **"workflow-rendered" vs "workflow-owned"** — FR-005 says "workflow-owned error string"; FR-022 says "workflow-rendered text"; NFR-051 says "workflow-rendered error notifications" and "workflow's own". Pick one of "workflow-owned" or "workflow-rendered" for the FR-051 family; the variants read like distinct concepts.
- **"context line" vs "context line in the result list"** — FR-013 introduces "per-object context line"; FR-033 says "result-list context line"; UJ-2 step 2 says "context line". Glossary lacks the term — consider defining once.
- **"primary name" vs "primary name attribute"** — FR-012 says "object's primary name attribute"; FR-013 says "primary name"; UJ-2 says "primary name". Minor; acceptable as elided form.
- **PAT vs Personal Access Token vs API token** — UJ-3 step 2 uses "Attio API token"; FR-022 says "Attio API token"; FR-024 uses "PAT"; glossary defines PAT. The user-facing strings ("API token") are consistent with the install copy; the spec body should use "PAT" everywhere it isn't a literal UI string. Currently mixed but defensible.
- **"⏎" rendering** — used everywhere; consistent.
- **"keystroke-Enter" (NFR-001)** — odd compound; elsewhere written as "press ⏎" or "Enter". → "after the Enter keystroke".
- **"⌘⏎ degrades" vs "this mod degrades"** — FR-015 uses "this mod" once. Drop "mod" terminology.
- **"sub-script-filter overlay" vs "sub-search overlay"** — FR-019 mentions "record-reference editing sub-search overlay (FR-042)"; FR-042 calls the same thing a "sub-script-filter" for single-select and a "sub-search" for record-reference. Pick one for the record-reference case.
- **"workspace member" vs "workspace member ID"** — consistent usage.
- **"in-place" vs "inline"** — §1 ¶3 says "in-place edit"; FR-005 says "in-place"; FR-041 says "inline ('Stage: Discovery — edit')"; FR-042 says "inline Alfred text input". Both terms are used correctly for different referents (mutation-without-leaving vs UI-element-inside-line) — no change needed, but worth noting they're easy to conflate.

## Grammar / typos

- **§1 ¶1** "Attio is a modern CRM whose users — sales reps, customer success, founders, recruiters — spend most of their day" — `customer success` as a role label is fine but lacks parallelism with the plural-rep forms. Consider "customer success reps".
- **§1 ¶2** "the macOS productivity launcher that already mediates" — "that" is correct as a restrictive relative; fine.
- **UJ-2 step 3** "the query field becomes the note body, Enter creates an Attio Note linked to this record" — comma splice (noted above).
- **UJ-3 step 4** "generate a new token granting the scopes V1 needs — read on records and tasks, write on tasks and notes, write on record attributes (for F-G updates) — and copy it" — long em-dash insertion; grammatical but heavy.
- **FR-002** "no `null` artefacts" — `artefacts` is British spelling; the rest of the doc uses American (`behavior`, `recognize` — actually neither appears, but `optimize`/`maximize` family absent). Mild; pick `artifacts` for consistency if American spelling is the target.
- **FR-005** "If the API call fails: the notification surfaces..." — colon-after-conditional is unusual but readable. Consider "On failure, the notification surfaces...".
- **FR-006** "Read-only." — single-word sentence is fine.
- **FR-008** "shows a single result with copy that names what was searched (e.g. 'No tasks due today for <workspace member name>')" — "copy" used as a noun (UX copy) is fine but slightly jargony; readers may parse it as the verb.
- **FR-012** "No fuzzy matching, no typo tolerance — surface this explicitly in docs." — sentence drifts into instruction mode mid-clause. Consider splitting: "No fuzzy matching, no typo tolerance. Documentation must surface this explicitly."
- **FR-019** "**Drill-down is single-level in V1**:" — bold on the leading phrase plus colon is fine, but the trailing rationale "Multi-level drill-down navigation is a V2 candidate (§9)" should match the doc-wide convention.
- **FR-025** "These values are persisted via `alfredClient.config` and cached until the token changes." — "persisted... and cached" implies two distinct operations; in practice they're one. Consider "...persisted via `alfredClient.config` and retained until the token changes."
- **FR-031** "Manual refresh via `attio:refresh` (FR-032) when an admin adds new options." — sentence fragment (no main verb). → "Manual refresh via `attio:refresh` (FR-032) is required when an admin adds new options."
- **FR-040** "Editable scalar attribute types on **records** (people, companies, deals): text (single and multi-line), ..." — list starts after a colon with no introducing verb. Acceptable in spec prose.
- **FR-046** "While the PATCH is in flight, the drill-down shows a loading indicator on the affected line." — fine.
- **NFR-001** "results appear in the Alfred bar in a single render frame after keystroke-Enter" — "keystroke-Enter" is malformed; either "after Enter" or "after the keystroke" (noted above).
- **NFR-004** "All TTLs are review candidates against real usage in V2." — "review candidates against" is awkward. → "All TTLs are subject to review against real usage in V2."
- **NFR-005** "exponential backoff and a single retry; on second failure, error item is shown" — missing article. → "on second failure, an error item is shown".
- **NFR-006a** "If two invocations race on the same cache row" — "cache row" is undefined ("entry" is used elsewhere). → "If two invocations race on the same cache entry".
- **NFR-009** "An opt-in anonymous telemetry path is a V2 candidate (§9) for unblocking quantitative success metrics." — "for unblocking" is awkward gerund-prep. → "...candidate (§9) to unblock quantitative success metrics."
- **NFR-010** "the `sindresorhus/conf` store used for cached identity / workspace slug / `cmd_enter_hint_dismissed` / token-hash" — slash list is inconsistent with the comma lists elsewhere in the doc.
- **§7 intro** "GitHub-Issue-derived community-signal proxies" — heavy compound. Acceptable; if simplifying, "GitHub-issue-derived community signals".
- **§7 M-3** "Volume of issues under `feature` and questions — engaged-user proxy." — "and questions" is unclear (is `questions` another label?). Either define or rephrase: "Volume of issues under `feature` and general questions — engaged-user proxy."
- **§8 ¶2** "the Alfred Gallery submission timing" — should be "Alfred Gallery submission timing" (no preceding "the") for parallelism with the other two items in the list.
- **§9 V2-9** "V2 should articulate a release cadence for adding new types as they ship" — "as they ship" pronoun ambiguity (the types or Attio?). → "...as Attio ships them."
- **Glossary `web_url`** "Tasks have no API-returned `web_url`; the workflow constructs one per FR-004." — semicolon joins two independent clauses correctly; fine.
